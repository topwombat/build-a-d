# Mach 1.5 transatlantic business jet: proposal (working draft)

Generated by `python -m ssbj proposal` from committed pipeline data; do not edit by hand.

Status: **concept stage, roadmap item 1 complete.** Evidence base: Concorde run `20261006T191624-74a9a6` (code `f3ed9ffe54`), 5 validation cases, 16 checks, **5 failing**.

## 1. What is proposed

- A Mach 1.5 business jet sized for transatlantic city pairs, designed with an open, reproducible pipeline in which every number carries a source and an error bar.
- Propulsion: a **bought gas-generator core** (HP compressor, combustor, HP turbine) with an in-house fan, LP turbine, forced mixer, nozzle and intake (D-024). Candidate cores, in order of technical fit: CFM56 / F101 '1/9' core (Affinity precedent, US export control), PW800 (GTF core), RR Pearl / BR700 (European, high HPC pressure ratio). Details: `ssbj/disciplines/propulsion/cores/CANDIDATES.md`.
- The aircraft itself is not yet sized (roadmap item 5). This draft states what the pipeline can currently predict and how well that has been checked.

## 4. Remaining deficiencies

### Model and evidence gaps

- The Concorde range pass still rests partly on offsetting errors: the engine model gives 23 % too much cruise thrust (helps the supersonic climb) and the engine weight is 44 % low (1,767 vs 3,175 kg). L/D (7.19 vs 7.14-7.5), TSFC (1.20 vs 1.195) and pre-cruise fuel (24 % vs 20 %) now agree.
- Two-spool engine model: specific thrust 15-27 % low on the Olympus, core work too high on the STCA turbofan (BPR +61 %). No duct or mixer losses, no offtakes, generic maps. 133 of the Concorde deck's in-envelope points did not converge and are neighbour-filled.
- Harris wave drag runs 16-29 % above the TM X-372 wind-tunnel data; no correction applied.
- No SSBJ design exists yet: sizing closure, constraint diagram and optimiser are roadmap item 5. The engine requirement is not yet an output (item 4).
- Low-speed (field length, Chapter 14 noise, vortex lift), trim, and sonic boom are not modelled (item 4).
- Only one aircraft-level validation case (Concorde). Mach 1.5-class cases (N+2 studies, AS2, F-16XL) are roadmap item 2.
- CI has not run on GitHub: waiting on the self-hosted runner labels from the owner.
- No human reviewer has signed off any validation case (item 7).

### Decisions needed from the owner

- Declared propulsion sigmas (thrust 5 %, SFC 5 %) are contradicted by both engine validations (Olympus thrust +23 %, STCA SFC -17 %). Proposal: widen to thrust 15 %, SFC 15 % until the cycle model passes, which widens the Concorde range interval. Not done without the owner, because it changes the gate after results are known.
- Mixer 'extraction ratio' in AIAA 2020-0263 is undefined; read as Pt_bypass / Pt_core (D-030). Confirm from an NPSS reference or the authors.
- Core choice for the SSBJ engine: CFM56/F101 '1/9' core is the best technical fit (Affinity precedent) but US-export-controlled; PW800 second; RR Pearl the European option but thermally hot. Supplier dialogue needed before the engine requirement is frozen.
- Concorde reserve definition (D-021, IFR 91.167) remains an assumption; ~700 nmi of range depends on it.

### Known limits of the methods (from the register)

Geometry:

- Thin-wing area-rule slicing: wing and fin thickness integrated along the cut line in their own planes (no dihedral, twist or camber).
- Nacelles are rectangular shells with the capture streamtube removed and cosine ramps at both ends; boundary-layer diverters and intake ramps are not modelled.
- Fuselage wetted area includes the wing-junction strip that OpenVSP removes (about 6 % on Concorde): conservative.
- Wing fuel volume (10-75 % chord) is a usable-volume proxy; fuselage and trim tanks are not counted.

Aerodynamics:

- Linear theory: invalid at high angle of attack where leading-edge vortex lift dominates (take-off, approach); no vortex lift is modelled.
- Transonic (0.9 < M < 1.2) wave drag is a fairing, not a prediction.
- Harris area rule assumes slender, smooth configurations; blunt bases, inlet spillage and boundary-layer diverters are not modelled.
- Harris wave drag is 16-29 % above wind-tunnel data on the NASA TM X-372 arrow wing-body (M 1.55-3.0); not corrected (no calibration on test data yet), inside the aero.wave sigma only at 1 to 1.2 sigma.
- No trim drag (stability module is Phase 4).
- No interference factors (Q = 1).

Propulsion:

- Generic compressor and turbine maps (pyCycle LPC/Fan/HPC/HPT/LPT) scaled to the design point.
- Dry: fixed nozzle throat area; reheat: nozzle opened to hold the LP compressor on its dry operating line. Real schedules differ.
- Validation shows errors larger than the declared 5 % sigmas: Olympus 593 (two-spool turbojet) cruise thrust +23 % and airflow +43 %; NASA STCA (bought-core turbofan) SFC -17 % and BPR +61 %. No duct or mixer pressure losses are modelled.
- Inlet recovery is the MIL-E-5008B standard schedule, not a designed intake.
- Reheat at a limited dry point is scaled from the T4_max reheat/dry ratio.
- Spillage, bypass, bleed and boat-tail drag not modelled; no customer power offtake.
- Idle and descent fuel flow extrapolated below 65 % of T4_max.

Weights:

- Regression database is subsonic transports; slender-delta supersonic structure (thin wing, kinetic heating, high-temperature alloys) is outside it.
- Wing equation uses gross reference area as the 'trapezoidal' area and the planform-average quarter-chord sweep; ogee and cranked planforms are not trapezoids.
- Engine dry weight comes from the propulsion module scaling law (30 % sigma).
- No variable-geometry intake or nozzle weight beyond the nacelle-group regression.
- Passenger seats and lavatories from Raymer Table 15.3 averages; galleys not included.

Mission:

- Climb, acceleration and descent follow the prescribed straight (Mach, altitude) paths; no trajectory optimisation.
- Flight-path angle is neglected in lift (L = W) and in distance (cos gamma = 1).
- Descent at idle: idle thrust taken as zero, idle fuel flow scaled from the SLS taxi setting by ambient pressure ratio.
- Diversion flown as level cruise at the reserve Mach and altitude (no climb or descent).
- No winds, no temperature deviation from ISA, no air-traffic constraints.
- Centre-of-gravity and trim drag effects (fuel transfer for trim) are not modelled.
- Reserve detail (alternate distance, hold) is an assumption with no error factor; on Concorde the reserves are ~19 t and removing hold and diversion adds ~700 nmi of range.
