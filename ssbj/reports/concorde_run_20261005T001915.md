## Warnings raised in this run

## Where these methods should not be trusted

- aerodynamics: Linear theory: invalid at high angle of attack where leading-edge vortex lift dominates (take-off, approach); no vortex lift is modelled.
- aerodynamics: Transonic (0.9 < M < 1.2) wave drag is a fairing, not a prediction.
- aerodynamics: Harris area rule assumes slender, smooth configurations; blunt bases, inlet spillage and boundary-layer diverters are not modelled.
- aerodynamics: No trim drag (stability module is Phase 4).
- aerodynamics: No interference factors (Q = 1).
- propulsion: Single-spool turbojet stands in for twin-spool engines; part-power matching is approximate.
- propulsion: Generic compressor and turbine maps (pyCycle AXI5, LPT2269) scaled to the design point.
- propulsion: No mechanical-speed or T2 limit schedule: only T4 is limited.
- propulsion: Inlet recovery is the MIL-E-5008B standard schedule, not a designed intake.
- propulsion: Spillage, bypass, bleed and nozzle boat-tail drag are not modelled.
- propulsion: Idle and descent fuel flow are extrapolated below 65 % of T4_max.
- weights: Regression database is subsonic transports; slender-delta supersonic structure (thin wing, kinetic heating, high-temperature alloys) is outside it.
- weights: Wing equation uses gross reference area as the 'trapezoidal' area and the planform-average quarter-chord sweep; ogee and cranked planforms are not trapezoids.
- weights: Engine dry weight comes from the propulsion module scaling law (30 % sigma).
- weights: No variable-geometry intake or nozzle weight beyond the nacelle-group regression.
- weights: Passenger seats and lavatories from Raymer Table 15.3 averages; galleys not included.
- mission: Climb, acceleration and descent follow the prescribed straight (Mach, altitude) paths; no trajectory optimisation.
- mission: Flight-path angle is neglected in lift (L = W) and in distance (cos gamma = 1).
- mission: Descent at idle: idle thrust taken as zero, idle fuel flow scaled from the SLS taxi setting by ambient pressure ratio.
- mission: Diversion flown as level cruise at the reserve Mach and altitude (no climb or descent).
- mission: No winds, no temperature deviation from ISA, no air-traffic constraints.
- mission: Centre-of-gravity and trim drag effects (fuel transfer for trim) are not modelled.
- mission: Reserve detail (alternate distance, hold) is an assumption with no error factor; on Concorde the reserves are ~19 t and removing hold and diversion adds ~700 nmi of range.
